## The bug

Both views measured a run's elapsed time as "now minus start", even after
the run had ended. At 13:00, for the 7-minute 01:00 run:

| View | Before (0.4.6) | After (0.4.7) |
|---|---|---|
| `/` budget line | `budget 8.0h · 12.0h elapsed · budget spent · started 01:00 AM`, full bar | `budget 8.0h · finished in 7m · started 01:00 AM`, short bar |
| `/live` time bar | `Time · 8h 00m in`, full bar | `Time · 7m in`, short bar |
| `/api/status` `overnight_run` | `elapsed_seconds` ≈ 43,000, `remaining_seconds` 0 | `finished: true`, `elapsed_seconds` 447, `remaining_seconds` null |

The misleading reading came after every short run, which is the case 0.4.4
fixed in `overnight` itself. That made a quick finish look like a run that
used its whole budget.

**Fix:**
- `dashboard.py`: `overnight_run` gains `finished`. It is true when the
  log has its `overnight done`/`aborting` line, or has gone quiet past
  `ACTIVE_LOG_WINDOW_SECONDS` (a killed run). A finished run is timed to
  the log's last write, and its `remaining_seconds` is null. The page shows
  "finished in …".
- `live.py`: a run that isn't alive is timed to its log's last write.
